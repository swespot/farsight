Farsight Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Farsight ("the App", "we", "us") is a Shopify application built and operated by Joachim Ekström that shows AI-powered product recommendations ("customers who liked this also liked") on a merchant's storefront. This policy explains what data Farsight processes, why, and how it is protected. It is written to be read by both the merchants who install Farsight ("you", "the merchant") and, where relevant, by the customers who visit merchant storefronts.

1. What data we process, and why

Farsight is designed to process the minimum data needed to generate and measure product recommendations. We do not collect customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, or shipping addresses.

From the merchant's product catalog (via the Shopify Admin API, using the write_products scope): product titles, descriptions, and prices. This is converted into a mathematical representation ("embedding") used to compute which products are similar to each other. No customer data is involved in this step.

From storefront visitors interacting with the recommendation widget: which product page or cart the widget was shown on, which products were shown, and whether a shown product was clicked or added to cart. This is stored as product IDs and interaction counts only — never tied to a visitor's name, account, or contact details.

From completed orders (via the Shopify read_orders scope and the orders/create webhook, used only to measure whether a recommendation led to a sale): for each order, we look only at whether a line item carries our own internal tag (added automatically when a shopper adds a recommended product to their cart) and, if so, record the product ID, the line item's price and quantity, the order ID, and the currency. We do not read, store, or process the customer's name, email, address, or any other personal information present on the order.

Analytics and consent: The click/impression tracking described above is implemented as a Shopify Web Pixel Extension, which Shopify only executes for a storefront visitor if that visitor has granted the "analytics" consent category (through the merchant's own cookie/consent banner, where one is configured). If a visitor has not granted that consent, Farsight's tracking code does not run for them at all — this is enforced by Shopify's platform, not by our own code.

2. Why we process this data

The sole purpose of the data described above is to: (a) generate relevant product recommendations, (b) let the merchant see, inside the Farsight admin dashboard, how many people viewed, clicked, added to cart, and purchased through a recommendation, and how much revenue it generated. We do not use this data for any other purpose, and we do not sell, rent, or otherwise share it with third parties.

3. Data retention

Analytics events (impressions, clicks, add-to-carts, and order attributions) are automatically deleted after 365 days. Product catalog embeddings are kept only for as long as the merchant has Farsight installed, and are deleted when the merchant uninstalls the app (via Shopify's app/uninstalled webhook).

4. Data security

All data in transit between the storefront, Shopify, and Farsight's servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Data at rest is stored in a database that is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider.

5. Third parties

Farsight uses OpenAI's embeddings API to compute product similarity. The text sent to OpenAI is limited to product titles and descriptions already public on the merchant's storefront — no customer or order data is ever sent to OpenAI.

Farsight does not, by itself, share data with any advertising or marketing platform (such as Meta/Facebook). If/when a merchant enables a supported ad-platform integration, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what is shared and under what consent conditions before that feature is enabled.

6. Merchant responsibilities

As the store owner, you remain responsible for maintaining your own privacy policy for your customers and for configuring a cookie/consent banner if required in the jurisdictions you sell into. Farsight's own tracking respects the consent signals from any such banner automatically, as described in Section 1.
