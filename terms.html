4. Data processing agreement

This section applies to the extent Farsight processes personal data on your behalf. You are the data controller and Farsight is your data processor. It applies alongside the description of data in the Privacy Policy.

4.1 Subject matter, nature and purpose. Farsight processes data solely to provide the service described in section 1: generating product recommendations and measuring whether they led to sales. We process it only on your documented instructions, which are the settings you choose in the app and these terms, and we do not use it for any other purpose. We do not sell it or share it with third parties other than the subprocessors in 4.5.

4.2 Categories of data and data subjects.

  • Your product catalog: product titles and descriptions (not personal data).
  • Storefront interaction events: which product pages and carts a recommendation was shown on and which products were shown, clicked or added to cart, stored as product IDs and counts only. They are collected only for visitors who have granted the analytics consent category in Shopify's consent framework.
  • Order attribution data: for order line items that carry Farsight's own tag, the product ID, price, quantity, order ID and currency.

Farsight does not collect or store customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, shipping or billing addresses, or any account or device identifiers of your customers. Farsight therefore does not knowingly process data that identifies a natural person, and the data subjects concerned are at most visitors to your storefront.

4.3 Duration. We process the data while Farsight is installed on your store. Interaction and order events are automatically deleted after 365 days. After you uninstall, Shopify sends the shop/redact compliance webhook (about 48 hours later) and we delete all data held for your shop. Backups of the database are kept for a limited period (currently five days) and then expire on their own; they are not restored or used for any other purpose.

4.4 Security. We protect the data with the following measures: encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS); encryption at rest (the database is stored on an encrypted volume); storing data per shop and deleting it per shop; limiting access to the data and to production systems to the developer, using account credentials that are not stored in the source code; and implementing Shopify's mandatory compliance webhooks (customers/data_request, customers/redact, shop/redact) and verifying the signatures of Shopify's webhooks and app proxy requests. The developer is bound to keep the data confidential.
